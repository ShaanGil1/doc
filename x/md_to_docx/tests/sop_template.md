5. **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

    b. **Office of Financial Services Activity (FSA) Lead:** Shall ensure all FSA procedures comply with DLA policy and provide guidance to subordinate personnel.
    c. **Division Chiefs and Section Chiefs:** Responsible for supervising the execution of FSA-related activities in their respective units to ensure compliance with this SOP.

6. **PROCEDURES:**
    a. **Overview:**  
    This section outlines the procedural steps for implementing FSA requirements at DLA to ensure standardized execution and compliance.
    b. **Description:**  
        (1) Identify FSA requirements applicable to the assigned activity.
        (2) Coordinate with relevant internal offices to obtain necessary resources and data.
        (3) Execute FSA tasks in alignment with DLA policies and financial regulations.
        (4) Document all FSA activities accurately and timely in prescribed systems or formats.
        (5) Review and report FSA compliance metrics as required.
    c. **Inputs/Outputs:**  
        (1) Inputs include FSA policy documents, financial data, tasking memoranda, and internal reporting requirements.
        (2) Outputs include completed financial reports, compliance status records, and audit documentation.
