**ENCLOSURE 2: RESPONSIBILITIES**

1. **DIRECTOR / CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, DLA (J8)**. Under the authority, direction, and control of the DLA Director, the Chief Financial Officer will:
    a. Serve as the Office of Primary Responsibility (OPR) for DLA accounting policies.
    b. Oversee the preparation, audit readiness, and timely submission of schedules.
