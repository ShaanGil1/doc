1. **PURPOSE:** This Instruction establishes policy, assigns responsibilities, and prescribes procedures for the accounting and reporting of fiduciary activities and deposit fund balances held by the Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) in accordance with references (a) and (b).
2. **SUMMARY OF CHANGES:**
    a. Adds quarterly reconciliation certification requirements.
    b. Removes the legacy monthly suspense report.
    c. Updates references to the current DoD 7000.14-R volumes.
3. **APPLICABILITY:**
This Instruction applies to all DLA activities that receive, hold, or disburse non-federal funds, including deposit funds, customer advances, and seized assets.
4. **DEFINITIONS**: See Glossary.
5. **POLICY:**
    a. It is DLA policy that fiduciary activities are accounted for separately from DLA's own assets and liabilities.
    b. Fiduciary balances will be reconciled to Treasury records at least quarterly and certified by the responsible official.
    c. Deposit funds held on behalf of others will not be used to finance DLA operations under any circumstance.
6. **RESPONSIBILITIES:** See Enclosure 2.
7. **PROCEDURES:** See Enclosure 3.
8. **INFORMATION REQUIREMENTS:**
    a. **Quarterly Certification:** Each holding activity submits the reconciliation certification to J8B within 15 business days after quarter end.
    b. **Annual Schedule:** J8B prepares the annual Schedule of Fiduciary Activity for the DLA Annual Financial Report.
9. **RELEASABILITY:** Cleared for public release. This Instruction is available on the DLA Issuances website.
10. **INTERNAL CONTROLS:** See Enclosure 4. The internal management control requirements in this Instruction are subject to evaluation, testing, and approval.
11. **EXPIRATION DATE:**
DLA will reissue or cancel this Instruction by the fifth anniversary of its effective publication date identified in the digital signature below. If not, it will automatically expire.

**ENCLOSURE 2: RESPONSIBILITIES**
1. **CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, DLA (J8).** Under the authority, direction, and control of the DLA Director, the Chief Financial Officer will:
    a. Serve as the Office of Primary Responsibility (OPR) for DLA fiduciary accounting policy.
    b. Oversee the preparation and timely submission of the Schedule of Fiduciary Activity.
    c. Ensure segregation of duties exists across DLA financial systems that hold fiduciary balances.
2. **DLA ACCOUNTING POLICY DIVISION (J8B).** Under the authority, direction, and control of the CFO, J8B will:
    a. Interpret federal accounting standards and issue implementing guidance.
    b. Perform annual reviews of all DLA fiduciary accounts.
        (1) Verify that each account has a designated responsible official.
        (2) Confirm that quarterly certifications were received and reconciled.
    c. Coordinate with the Defense Finance and Accounting Service (DFAS) on journal entries affecting fiduciary accounts.
3. **HOLDING ACTIVITY COMMANDERS AND DIRECTORS.** Commanders and directors of activities that hold fiduciary balances will:
    a. Designate in writing a responsible official for each fiduciary account.
    b. Submit the quarterly reconciliation certification to J8B.

**ENCLOSURE 3: PROCEDURES**
1. **Identification of Fiduciary Activity.**
    a. On receipt of non-federal funds, the holding activity determines whether the funds meet the definition of fiduciary activity in reference (a).
    b. If they do, the funds are recorded in a deposit fund account and excluded from DLA's own financial statements.
2. **Quarterly Reconciliation.**
    a. The responsible official reconciles the account balance to Treasury records.
    b. Differences are researched and cleared or documented as follows:
        (1) Timing differences are annotated and carried forward one quarter.
        (2) Unexplained differences older than one quarter are reported to J8B.
    c. The certification is signed and submitted within 15 business days after quarter end.
3. **Annual Reporting.**

| Deliverable | Prepared by | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule of Fiduciary Activity | J8B | 15 October |
| Schedule of Fiduciary Net Assets | J8B | 15 October |
| Deposit fund footnote | J8B with DFAS | 1 November |

**ENCLOSURE 4: INTERNAL CONTROLS**
1. The quarterly reconciliation and certification is a key internal control and is subject to annual testing.
2. Segregation of duties between recording, reconciling, and certifying fiduciary transactions is required.
3. Results of internal control testing are reported through the DLA Managers' Internal Control Program.

**PART II. DEFINITIONS**
Deposit Fund: An account established to hold funds that do not belong to the federal government, pending disposition.
Fiduciary Activity: The collection or receipt, and the management, protection, accounting, investment and disposition by the federal government of cash or other assets in which non-federal individuals or entities have an ownership interest.
Responsible Official: The individual designated in writing to reconcile and certify a fiduciary account.
