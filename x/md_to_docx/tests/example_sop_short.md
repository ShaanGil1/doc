__DLA STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE__
SOP 4140.01-A

__OPR:__ Logistics Operations, Inventory Policy Branch (J341)
__SUBJECT:__ Cycle Counting of Warehouse Inventory
__REFERENCES:__ DLAI 4140.01, "Inventory Management," current edition; DoD 4140.01, Volume 11

1. __PURPOSE:__ This SOP prescribes the steps for conducting and documenting cycle counts of on-hand inventory at DLA Distribution centers.
2. __SUMMARY OF CHANGES:__ Initial issuance.
3. __APPLICABILITY:__ All DLA Distribution centers and any contractor-operated storage site holding DLA-owned material.
4. __DEFINITIONS:__ See Glossary.
5. __RESPONSIBILITIES:__ See Enclosure 1.
6. __PROCEDURES:__
  (1) A count schedule is generated at the start of each fiscal quarter.
  (2) Counters are assigned locations they do not normally stock.
  (3) Each location is counted blind, without visibility of the system balance.
    (a) Discrepancies greater than the tolerance in Enclosure 2 are recounted the same day.
    (b) Confirmed discrepancies are adjusted and the cause documented.
  (4) Results are posted within one business day of the count.
7. __INFORMATION REQUIREMENTS:__ The monthly count accuracy summary is submitted to J341 by the 10th of the following month.
8. __RELEASABILITY:__ Unlimited.
9. __INTERNAL CONTROLS:__ Count accuracy is a key performance indicator reviewed monthly. Adjustments over the dollar threshold in Enclosure 2 require second-level approval.
10. __EXPIRATION DATE:__ This SOP expires five years from the date of signature unless reissued or cancelled.

**Enclosure 1 - Responsibilities**
1. **Director, DLA Distribution:** owns the cycle count program and approves the annual count plan.
2. **Center Commanders:** ensure counts are scheduled, staffed, and completed.
3. **Inventory Accuracy Officers:** validate recounts and approve adjustments within their authority.

**Enclosure 2 - Tolerances and Thresholds**
> Tolerances apply per location and per count event, not per item.

1. Quantity tolerance: plus or minus two percent of the system balance, or one unit, whichever is greater.
2. Dollar threshold for second-level approval: any single adjustment over $5,000 extended value.
3. Locations that fail two consecutive counts are moved to the monthly count schedule.

**PART II: DEFINITIONS**
Blind Count: A physical count performed without the counter having access to the recorded balance.
Cycle Count: A recurring count of a subset of locations, scheduled so that all locations are counted within a defined period.
