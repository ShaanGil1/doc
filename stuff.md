# Writer Agent - Client Discovery Questions

## Specialist Context

- We understand that DLA has specialists in different areas and that specialization determines which policies a writer is assigned. Can you walk us through the different specialist types, how many there are, and what the key differences are in their workflow or reference documents?
- Does the specialist type change which reference documents matter, which keywords to search for, which style rules apply, and how the writer approaches the work?

## The Reference Brief Form

1. What are all of the fields in the form that gets filled out before starting a policy draft? Which fields are required and which are optional? Can we see a completed example?
2. When creating a policy from an existing one, are the prior policies and all of their references included and known upfront? What information is given about why the change is needed, and what are the key things you look for when handling a revision versus a net-new policy?
3. On the inverse, when there is no pre-existing policy to work from, where do you start? What is the process for building a policy from scratch and how does that differ from a revision?

## Style Writer and Style Rules

1. We know Style Writer is available as a tool, but how can we pull information from it? Is there an API where we can extract its rules or outputs programmatically, or would we need to run it ourselves and derive the rules from its behavior?
2. You have mentioned rules that should be prioritized, such as active vs. passive voice, grammar, and spelling. Are there other stylistic choices you look for that are not covered by Style Writer, and do you have a priority list of those rules whether they are in or out of the style guide?
3. You have also mentioned cases where Style Writer flags things you disagree with, like "complex wording" when the wording is actually standard terminology in your domain. Is there a way for us to still use that rule while allowing the domain-specific terms that you commonly use? How would we know which terms to whitelist, and does this differ by specialist area?

## Quality and Review Process

1. What are the biggest red flags and most common issues you look for when reviewing a policy document? When you first open a document to review it, where do your eyes go and what do you check first? We recognize this may differ from person to person or by specialist area, so any perspective helps.

## Reference Documents and Search Behavior

1. You have mentioned that you specifically look at NFR and SFASS. Are there additional core documents beyond those, and could the set of core documents differ over time or by specialist type?
2. You mentioned that you commonly Ctrl+F for specific keywords in documents. What are those keywords? We expect they differ by document type and specialization, so ideally we would map those out per type. Are there specific terms that constantly come up, and are there specific parts of a document where you tend to look for them?
3. When you need to find a relevant document, how are you searching for it? Do you have a mental model of which documents you need and where they live, or is there a search engine or system you use to locate them? Does this differ by specialist area?
