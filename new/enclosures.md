# References
The following authorities apply to this SOP.

## Statutory and Regulatory
Title 49: Governs the transportation of Agency materiel by commercial carrier.

Defense Transportation Regulation: Establishes discrepancy reporting standards
across the Department.

## Agency Issuances
- Agency Instruction 4140.01, Inventory Management
- Agency Manual 4500.03, Transportation Operations

# Reporting Timelines
Ownership: The transportation office owns the case file from discovery through
closure.

## Initial Report
The initiating activity performs the following steps in order.

1. Confirm the discrepancy against the shipping document.
2. Photograph the shipment and packaging before it is disturbed.
3. Submit the TDR through the system of record.

## Escalation
Threshold: Any discrepancy valued above the review threshold is escalated to
the regional transportation officer within one business day.

| Value | Reviewer | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Under $2,500 | Local office | 10 days |
| $2,500 to $25,000 | Regional officer | 5 days |
| Over $25,000 | Headquarters | 2 days |

# Investigation
Casework: The assigned investigator gathers evidence, interviews the receiving
activity, and documents findings in the case file.

## Evidence
Photographs, the signed shipping document, and the carrier's response together
form the minimum evidentiary record.

## Findings
A finding must state whether the carrier, the shipper, or the receiving
activity bears responsibility.

# Closeout
Closure: A case is closed when the finding is recorded and any claim is either
paid or formally denied.

## Records Retention
Case files are retained for six years following closure.
